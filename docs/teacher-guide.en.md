# Teacher's guide

Toccata lets you **prepare** an activity (a script of steps), have the class **play** it (in groups or alone), **steer it live**, then **review** the session.
It runs in a browser, on tablets and computers, and keeps working offline.

## 1. Getting started
1. **Create your account**: *Create a teacher account* (your administrator gives you the invitation code).
2. **Create a class**: *Classes* → *New class*, paste the student names (one per line) → *Create accounts*. **Usernames and passphrases are shown once**: print the sheet (login QR included). Forgotten? *New passphrase*.
3. Students sign in from any device with their username and six-word passphrase.

## 2. Write an activity
*My activities* → *New activity*. A **script** is a linear sequence of **steps**: add, rename and reorder them (drag and drop or keyboard); the **eye** hides a step from students (that is how you make variants);
write the **instructions** in rich text; add **resources** (links, files) and **apps** — timer, kanban board, shared text, form (built-in editor), external web app — to a step or to the whole activity with the *Add* wizard;
a step can be **blocked** until a form is filled in. The preview is faithful: it is the students' interface.

## 3. Distribute
*Distribute*: *One per student*, *By groups* (drag students) or *Whole class*, before **or during** the session.

## 4. Steer live
*Monitoring*: one tile per group (step, coloured timer, items, help requests), live. **Observe** (read-only mirror); actions on all groups or the ones you tick: **extend the timer**, broadcast a **message**,
**get attention** (freezes screens), **lock/unlock** a step, give **feedback**; **projector mode**; one-handed **remote control** on a phone; **edit live** from the editor and choose who the change applies to.

## 5. Reuse and share
**Export** an activity (`.toccata` file), **import** it, **duplicate** it. **Templates**: *Share as template* publishes the script (no files, nothing about students) for other teachers; *Templates* → *Use this template* creates your own copy.

## 6. Review
*Review*: **planned vs actual** table per step (planned duration from timers, median actual duration, groups reached, help requests, hand-ins, live changes, variants), plus three private reflection questions kept with the activity.

## 7. Research and personal data
The **log export** (CSV/JSON) is pseudonymised, holds no names or student work, and requires you to confirm consent was collected. The **Privacy** page lets anyone download their data; from a class page you can **export** a student's data or **delete** the student (their work is erased).

## 8. In class without Internet
If your school runs a **class server**, prepare classes and activities **beforehand** (online); in the room everyone connects to that server, which resyncs later. The top-right indicator shows the sync state.
Without a class server, the app keeps your work on the device and sends it when the network returns.
